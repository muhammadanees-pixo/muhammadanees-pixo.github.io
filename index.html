Wiggly Worm: Snake Puzzle

A cozy snake puzzle game about a little worm with a big appetite. Guide it through hand-made mazes and reach the heart.

Coming soon on Google Play

About the game

Your worm can't turn back on itself, so every move counts. Plan your path, eat apples to grow longer, push rocks onto buttons, cut your worm in two and switch between the halves. The 23 levels bring in new ideas one at a time, and the later mazes are real brain teasers.

23 hand-made levels

Gentle start, clever finish. Earn up to three stars on every level.

New ideas as you play

Apples, strawberries, currents, rocks, doors, scissors and fragile eggs.

Dress up your worm

Worm colors, hats, glasses, tails, trails and cute pets.

Never stuck for long

Undo, restart, or watch a hint play the next few moves.
