Privacy Policy

Wiggly Worm: Snake Puzzle (Android)
Effective date: 16 October 2026

In short: Wiggly Worm has no accounts and no sign-in, and we (PIXO LABS) do not collect your personal information on our own servers. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads, and our advertising partners (Unity and Google) collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as explained below. Purchases are handled by Google Play. You can change your ad choices at any time.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the Game saves the following only on your device:

  • your level progress, stars, pearls, unlocked items and the outfit your worm wears;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, on-screen controls);
  • whether you own the "Remove Ads" purchase;
  • your advertising consent choice (if you were asked for one).

We do not receive this information. Android may include it in your device's own backup to your Google account, if you have turned backup on. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the Game or by clearing its storage (Android Settings → Apps → Wiggly Worm → Storage → Clear storage).

3. What we do not collect

We do not ask for, and the Game does not collect for us, your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location, microphone or camera. The Game has no chat and no way to share content with other players. We do not run our own analytics or tracking.

Information our ad partners collect

When the Game requests or shows an ad, these partners may collect, directly from your device:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location, such as your country or city;
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type and the Game's name and version;
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown, watched or tapped;
  • Diagnostic information about how the ad software performs;
  • Purchase history signals, which some ad partners may use for advertising and analytics.

They use this information to show ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure ads, for analytics, to prevent fraud and keep their services secure and, where the law allows or you have agreed, to show personalized ads. This information is sent to them over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Each partner handles it under its own privacy policy, linked above.

Your ad choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in one of these regions, the Game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the Game: Settings → More → Privacy Choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings (for example Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Inside an ad: some ads include a privacy icon that lets you see or limit how the ad partner uses your data.
  • Remove Ads: if you buy "Remove Ads", the Game stops showing ads and no longer starts the advertising software.

The Game does not show ads when it runs on Google's automated test devices.

5. Purchases

The Game offers one optional in-app purchase, "Remove Ads". Payments are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details. Google Play tells the Game only whether the purchase is complete, so the Game can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall. Google's handling of your payment data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over, and it is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and the Game has no accounts, chat or ways to share personal information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help, including by contacting our ad partners.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: game data stays until you uninstall the Game or clear its storage.
  • With us: we do not store personal information on our servers. If you email us, we keep your email only as long as needed to answer you, and delete it on request.
  • With our ad partners: they keep data according to their own policies (linked in section 4). To limit or remove data linked to your device, reset or delete your Android advertising ID. You can also contact Unity at DPO@unity3d.com or through the links in their policy, and Google through Google Account → Data & privacy.
  • Ask us: to request deletion or access, email muhammadanees.pixo@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days and help you reach our partners.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not hold personal information about you ourselves, most requests concern our ad partners; we will help you make them.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland): personalized advertising is based on your consent; showing non-personalized ads, measuring ads and preventing fraud are based on legitimate interests; processing a purchase is necessary to provide what you bought.

United States: we do not sell personal information for money. Some US state laws may treat the sharing of device identifiers with ad partners for targeted advertising as a "sale" or "sharing". You can opt out by deleting your advertising ID or opting out of ads personalization in Android settings, or by visiting Unity's "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" page, and you can contact us with any request.

10. Security and international transfers

Our partners send data over encrypted connections. They may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, with the safeguards described in their policies.

11. Changes to this policy
